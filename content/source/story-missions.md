# Story: Missions

Each secret mission tells its own story. The Opening plays when a player keeps the mission. Each milestone has a beat that plays when it's claimed (milestones can come in any order, so each beat stands on its own), and the Finale plays when the mission is complete. Beats are private until the end of the game, when every adventurer's tale is read out. `{name}` becomes the adventurer's name.

## One-skull missions

- Opening: The Moth Queen hasn't slept in nine nights, and the whole Meadow is yawning. A sleepy moth lands on {name}'s sleeve with a message: find a way to sing her down.
- Milestone 1: The Meadow hums under {name}'s feet. The flowers here remember every lullaby ever sung, and they teach {name} the first verse.
- Milestone 2: Resting twice, {name} learns the secret of lullabies: you have to know how to be still before you can make anyone else be still.
- Milestone 3: A creature curls up beside {name} and falls asleep instantly. The lullaby is working, at least on small things.
- Finale: With a friend at their side, {name} sings to the Moth Queen. Her wings fold, the meadow sighs, and for the first time in nine nights, everyone sleeps.

- Finale: {name} carries the ruin-treasure deep into the forest, and the old roots close around it like a hand. The Rootkeeper smiles. The errand is done.
