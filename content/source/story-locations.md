# Story: Locations

What happens at each location, told as a short scene. The game shows the scene first and the card's result underneath it. `{name}` becomes the adventurer's name. Success and Fail lines play after the roll; a Spark line plays after the Success line when a Spark triggers. Rest options have one Scene line.

## Forest

### Whispering Glade

**Listen Closely**
- Success: {name} presses an ear to the bark. The oaks are gossiping, and one of them is very sure it knows what a rival is after.
- Fail: The trees fall silent the moment {name} leans in, then burst into rustling laughter that goes on far too long.
- Spark: A voice deeper than the rest whispers about what the Bloom will do next.

**Gather Fallen Acorns**
- Success: {name} finds an acorn that hums faintly in the palm, as warm as a held breath.
- Fail: Every acorn {name} picks up is hollow. A squirrel watches from a branch, saying nothing.
- Spark: Another acorn drops straight into {name}'s hand, as if the tree meant it.

**Nap Against an Old Oak**
- Scene: {name} settles against a trunk that murmurs old lullabies, and wakes with leaves in their hair and the ache gone from their bones.

### Glowcap Hollow

**Pick Glowcaps**
- Success: {name} breathes on a glowcap until it shines like a lantern, then pinches it free.
- Fail: That was not a glowcap. {name}'s skin goes green, then slick, then {name} is a frog.
- Spark: The spores settle on {name}'s tongue and fizz like a tiny thunderstorm.

**Follow the Glow**
- Success: The light leads down a hidden dip to a mushroom with a pearl where its gills should be.
- Fail: The glow winks out mid-step, and {name} tumbles into a patch of very prickly caps.
- Spark: For a moment the whole forest glows, and {name} can see the shape of every path ahead.

**Rest in the Soft Light**
- Scene: {name} curls up in the hollow while the mushrooms pulse slowly, like the forest breathing.

### The Woodcutter's Camp

**Search the Packs**
- Success: Under a bedroll, {name} finds a purse the woodcutter forgot in their hurry.
- Fail: The pack is already occupied. A raccoon objects, loudly and with teeth.
- Spark: Leaning against the woodpile is a fine axe, its handle worn smooth by honest work.

**Chop Firewood for Travelers**
- Success: {name} splits a neat stack of logs, and passing travelers pay well for a warm night.
- Fail: The wood is stubborn, the axe is dull, and {name} has blisters to show for it.
- Spark: Each swing lands truer than the last, and {name} feels their arms remember it.

**Share the Stew**
- Scene: The pot is still warm. {name} eats slowly by the embers and feels the bruises fade.

### Mossback Tortoise Crossing

**Ride Along**
- Success: Old Mossback lowers his great head and lets {name} climb aboard. He has decided they are friends.
- Fail: Mossback sneezes, a sound like a landslide, and {name} spends a while picking moss out of their ears.
- Spark: Mossback picks up the pace, which for him is a stroll, and carries {name} somewhere with a better view.

**Scrape the Moss**
- Success: {name} scrapes a strip of ancient moss from the shell. It smells like a thousand rainy afternoons.
- Fail: Mossback notices. His slow, offended stare is somehow worse than a bite, and then he bites.
- Spark: Tucked in the moss are coins dropped by some earlier, less careful rider.

**Wait for Him to Pass**
- Scene: {name} sits by the path and waits for the tortoise to finish crossing. It is restful. It is also very, very long.
