# Explore Cards

The prototype pool has 63 cards: 54 unique locations, 9 of which appear twice. Duplicates are the most common sights in Thaloroot. Players have a Meeting when they pick locations in the same region with the same skull rating (see Interaction Rules). After Playtest 2, ten options were changed to Spark so magic-focused characters have more to do.

## How to read a card

- **Teaser:** name, tags, skull rating, and one mystery line. This is all a player sees before choosing.
- **Options:** each lists its stat, the successes needed, and the stamina cost, followed by its Success, Fail, and Spark results. Rest and no-roll options cost nothing.
- **Spark:** triggered by any 6 on a successful roll. A few results need two or more 6s and say so.
- **Mishap:** two or more 1s. Unless a card names its own Mishap, it costs 1 stamina.
- **Treasure values** are shown as coin / VP. Cash a treasure for coin or keep it for VP at the end, never both.

| Item type | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Treasure | Cash for coin or keep for VP |
| Companion | A creature ally with an ongoing bonus |
| Gear | An ongoing bonus for the rest of the game |
| Food | Used once for a quick effect |
| Light source | Needed by some missions; most can also be cashed |

| Region | Unique cards | Total with copies | One skull | Two skulls | Three skulls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | 11 | 13 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Meadow | 11 | 13 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Swamp | 11 | 13 | 3 | 5 | 3 |
| Mountain | 10 | 12 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Ruins | 11 | 12 | 4 | 4 | 3 |

Creature and Peril are secondary tags spread across the regions.

## Open questions

- Every item named on these cards needs a single item list with its type and value. That's the next piece of the kit.
- Learning a rival's mission tag appears often. Playtest whether secret missions still feel secret.
- Reroll and redraw items (Lucky Thimble, Lucky Button, Fairy Charm, Wishbone) appear only as Spark results or minor rewards. Check that they still feel rare.
- Three-skull failures cost 3 health, half of a starting 6. That may be too punishing, or exactly right.
- What happens when the explore deck runs out mid-game: reshuffle the discards?
