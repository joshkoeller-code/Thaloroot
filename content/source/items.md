# Items

Every item named on the explore cards, plus the setup redraw items. This list is the source of truth for each item's rarity, value, and power.

## How items work

**Three kinds of power**

- **Reusable:** stays in your pack. Some powers are always on; others work once per game.
- **One use:** using it spends the item, and you lose its coin and VP value.
- **None:** a trade good. Cash it, keep it for VP, or trade it.

A treasure with a one-use power is the game's biggest small decision: cash it, keep it for VP, or spend it for its effect.

**Four rarities**

| Rarity | Copies in the game | Digital color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Many; several locations give the same item | Gray |
| Uncommon | A few | Green |
| Rare | 1 or 2 | Blue |
| Legendary | Exactly 1 | Gold |

**Other rules**

- Companions aren't items. They can't be stolen, cashed, or traded; they only leave by fleeing.
- Items that class abilities make (the Witch's potions, the Cook's food, the Blacksmith's reforged gear) follow the same rules: potions and food are one use, and reforged gear is reusable.
- **Merchants****:** each Merchant World card stocks 2 commons, 1 uncommon, and sometimes 1 rare. An item costs its coin value + 2, or + 4 for rares. Gear and food with no coin value cost 3 (common), 5 (uncommon), or 9 (rare).
- **Carryover (proposed):** only rare and legendary items can carry into future games, except Taproot Fragments.

## Taproot Fragments

The rarest and most powerful items in Thaloroot, able to turn the tide of a game.

- **Can't be sold, cashed, traded, or stolen.** Dealing in fragments is forbidden and dangerous, and a fragment burns any hand but the one that found it.
- **Hidden until used.** The finder gets only a cryptic whisper. The digital version draws the power at random from the pool below and reveals it on use.
- **Hold or unleash.** An unused fragment is worth 5 VP at game end.
- **It returns to the earth.** When the Bloom ends, every fragment sinks back into the roots. Fragments never carry into future games.
- **The price:** thal remembers greed. After using a fragment, you can't push your luck for the rest of the game.

| Fragment | Whisper when found | Power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragment of Becoming | "It remembers what you're meant to be." | Instantly claim one unclaimed milestone of your mission |
| Fragment of Root Sight | "It sees through every secret." | Secretly see every rival's mission |
| Fragment of the Turning Season | "It hums of things to come." | Look through the entire World deck and rearrange it; you may move the Finale up or down by up to 3 cards |
| Fragment of Exchange | "It wants what others carry." | Swap your entire pack with any rival's pack; companions stay put |
| Fragment of the Surge | "It's warm, and getting warmer." | For your next 3 turns, every 5 or 6 counts as two successes |
| Fragment of Deep Growth | "It's still growing." | Permanently gain +1 to any stat, ignoring the per-game growth limit |
| Fragment of the Rewilding | "It longs for the wild." | Clear every trap, haunt, hex, and seal in the game, then set 2 traps for free |
| Fragment of the Gardener-Kings | "It remembers a crown." | Choose any explore card from the deck or discards and play it this turn with +3 dice |
