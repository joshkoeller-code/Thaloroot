# Class Roster — Competitive Adventure RPG

 · 

27 classes are in the pool, and all of them can appear in any game through the class draft. Fourteen are fully built and thirteen are one-line concepts. Use the comparison table to scan, then the detail sections to judge.

## Core systems recap

Every class ability below plugs into these systems.

- **Base stats (5):** Brawn, Wits, Charm, Spark, Luck. Permanent, capped at 6, and they grow between games.
- **Rolls:** a pool of d6 equal to the stat, plus class, item, and ally dice. Each 5 or 6 is a success; options need 1 to 3 successes.
- **Sparks and Mishaps:** each 6 can trigger a bonus effect. Two or more 1s trigger a funny-bad Mishap.
- **Push your luck:** once per roll, add 2 dice after rolling, accepting more Mishap risk.
- **Stamina:** harder options cost stamina; resting restores it. Once per turn, you can pay 2 coin for trail rations to restore 2 stamina.
- **Payouts:** treasure is spent as coin or kept as VP, never both. Any success can instead be taken as a Lesson.
- **Lessons:** 5 Lessons in a stat raise it by 1 permanently; at most +2 per game.
- **Classes:** chosen fresh each game. Stat changes last only that game.
- **Interaction:** traps, haunts, and hexes target location names; Meetings (players at locations with the same region and skull rating) unlock brawls, trades, and team-ups.

## Health and being Downed

No player is ever eliminated; going down costs treasure and tempo instead.

- **Health:** everyone starts with 6. Classes may adjust it.
- **Damage:** from failed options, monsters, brawls, and traps.
- **Healing:** rest options, the Cook's Hearty Stew, the Witch's Mending potion, and later the Cleric.
- **Downed (0 health):** drop a random item at that location name, where the next player to draw it can claim it. Skip your next explore. Return with half health, rounded up.
- **Optional:** being Downed occasionally leaves a permanent quirk, such as Sees Stars (+1 die on Spark rolls, -1 on Wits).

These numbers are starting points to adjust in playtesting.

## Setup flow

Every class is available every game, and class comes before mission, so a player's class reveals little about their secret goal.

First, the group picks a game length (Quick, Standard, or Long), which sets the World deck size.

1. **Character:** build a new character (see the Characters tab) or load a returning one.
2. **Class draft:** each player is dealt 3 classes from the shared pool and picks 1. No two players can hold the same class. Newer or lower-renown characters see 4 options, except at 7 or 8 players, where everyone sees 3. Classes are public.
3. **Mission draft:** each player is dealt 3 missions and secretly picks 1. Proposed: a mission that fits a class poorly pays a small VP bonus for the extra challenge.
4. **Begin play.**

**Player count:** 2 to 8 players. The sweet spot is 3 to 6. At 7 or 8 players (party mode), the class draft gives everyone 3 options, the digital version resolves explore cards simultaneously, and Quick or Standard length is recommended.

The digital launch can ship with the 14 fully built classes, with concept classes added in later updates.

### Redraws and rerolls come from rare items

There is no built-in redraw. Players earn that power by finding and keeping special items, which keeps it rare. Example items:

| Item | Effect | Carries between games |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fortune-Teller's Deck | Redraw your class options once at setup, then it's spent | Yes |
| Two-Headed Coin | Redraw your mission options once at setup, then it's spent | Yes |
| Lucky Thimble | Reroll up to 2 dice once per game | Yes |
| Wishbone | Redraw both explore teasers once, then it snaps | No |

## Class anatomy and balance checks

Every class uses the same five parts, so any class can be learned in about a minute.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Stats this game | Two +1s and one -1 (or an equivalent swing) for this game only |
| Passive | Always-on rule that defines the play style |
| Active | Costs stamina or a class resource; usable most turns |
| Signature | Once per game; the big dramatic moment |
| Weakness | A real cost that creates tension, not just a smaller number |

Since any class can be dealt in any game, every class must pass these balance checks:

- It can complete any mission tag, even if slowly.
- Its weakness matters in most games.
- It doesn't dominate one payout (VP, coin, or growth) so hard that others can't compete.
- Its information or denial powers have at least one counter elsewhere in the pool.
- It stays fun against any other class, since any pairing can appear.

## Adventurer classes (fully built)

### Bramblebeast Tamer

*"Why fight it when you could keep it?"*

- **Stats this game:** +1 Charm, +1 Brawn, -1 Spark
- **Passive, Beast Bond:** +2 dice on any Creature-tagged option. Keep up to 3 companions instead of the usual 2.
- **Active, Sic 'Em (2 stamina):** a companion guards a location; the next rival who picks it faces the creature first.
- **Signature, Stampede:** all companions act at once, +1 die each on a single roll.
- **Weakness:** taking 2+ damage in one turn makes a companion flee.
- **Plays like:** builds a menagerie, controls locations, wins brawls with backup.

### Lantern-Hour Thief

*"Finders keepers. Also, takers keepers."*

- **Stats this game:** +1 Wits, +1 Luck, -1 Brawn
- **Passive, Light Fingers:** when you steal, you choose the item. +1 die at night, fog, and dark locations.
- **Active, Sneaky Traps (0 stamina):** set traps for free; up to 2 active.
- **Signature, The Swap:** secretly trade one item with any player; they learn it was you at round's end.
- **Weakness:** merchants know your face, so everything costs +1 coin.
- **Plays like:** sabotage, deduction, and grabbing the exact item a rival's mission needs.

### Toadstool Witch

*"Every mistake is just an ingredient."*

- **Stats this game:** +1 Spark, +1 Wits, -1 Charm
- **Active, Brew (1 stamina):** turn one treasure into a potion: Swiftness (+2 dice), Mending (heal 3), or Ribbit (a rival becomes a frog for a round).
- **Passive, Mishap Mastery:** your Mishaps don't trigger; gain 1 Lesson instead.
- **Signature, Hex the Path:** curse a location name; whoever picks it next rolls 2 Trouble dice.
- **Weakness:** potions eat treasure, so hoarding for VP hurts.
- **Plays like:** turns bad luck into growth and loot into tricks.

### Fox-Masked Trickster

*"It's only funny if you don't see it coming."* Alternate names: Rubber-Chicken Jester, Mirror-Coat Trickster.

- **Stats this game:** +1 Wits, +1 Charm, -1 Luck
- **Passive, Bluff:** when you claim a milestone, you may also drop a fake one.
- **Active, Switcheroo (1 stamina):** after everyone picks, swap your explore card with another player's.
- **Signature, Mistaken Identity:** for one round, your actions show on the TV under another player's name.
- **Weakness:** you can't receive Assist dice, and your Mishaps hit twice.
- **Plays like:** misdirection that makes the deduction game miserable for everyone else.

### Lantern-Bound Ghost

*"I'm not lost. I'm just not done yet."* Alternate names: Unfinished-Business Spirit, Teacup Ghost, Echo Specter.

- **Stats this game:** +1 Spark, +1 Luck, Brawn drops to 1
- **Passive, Incorporeal:** can't be stolen from in brawls; traps catch you only on a roll of 1 to 3.
- **Active, Haunt (1 stamina):** haunt a location name; whoever picks it rolls -1 die and you gain 1 coin.
- **Signature, Possession:** when a rival draws 2 explore cards, you choose which one they get.
- **Weakness:** carry only 3 items.
- **Plays like:** slippery, indirect pressure; every hoard-or-spend choice is tense.

### Pillow-Prophet Dreamweaver

*"I saw this in a dream. You lose."* Alternate names: Sleepwalking Oracle, Moth-Winged Dreamer, Yawning Seer.

- **Stats this game:** +1 Spark, +1 Luck, -1 Wits
- **Resource, Dream tokens:** earned by resting, spent on dream powers.
- **Passive, Lucid Rest:** a rest or no-roll option grants 1 Dream token, and rivals can't target you that round.
- **Dream Peek (1 Dream):** see a full card behind its teaser before choosing.
- **Dream Walk (1 stamina + 1 Dream):** learn one tag of a rival's mission, or add a Trouble die to their next roll.
- **Signature, The Shared Dream:** for one round, 1s count as successes and 6s become Mishaps for everyone.
- **Weakness:** at 0 stamina you fall asleep and skip a turn, waking with 2 Dream tokens.
- **Plays like:** a patient information master who strikes at the right moment.

## Trade classes (fully built)

These four make things, which gives the treasure economy an engine: other players can pay them for services.

### Gargoyle-Carver Stonemason

- **Stats this game:** +1 Brawn, +1 Wits, -1 Luck
- **Passive, Old Stones:** +2 dice at Ruins locations.
- **Active, Build a Wall (2 stamina):** seal a location name for one round; no one else can pick it.
- **Signature, Living Gargoyle:** a stone guardian attacks the next rival who lands where you place it.
- **Weakness:** heavy tools, so max stamina is 1 lower.

### Treehouse Carpenter

- **Stats this game:** +1 Brawn, +1 Charm, -1 Spark
- **Passive, Woodwise:** +2 dice at Forest locations.
- **Active, Build a Bridge (2 stamina):** revisit any location you've already explored.
- **Signature, The Walking Cottage:** rest anywhere for full stamina and stash items where no one can steal them.
- **Weakness:** every build costs 1 coin.

### Ember-Hearted Blacksmith

- **Stats this game:** +1 Brawn, +1 Spark, -1 Charm
- **Passive, Reforge:** turn treasure into gear (+1 die to a stat for the rest of the game); gear has no coin or VP value.
- **Service, Smithing for Hire:** rivals can pay you coin to reforge their items.
- **Signature, Masterwork:** one item becomes an Heirloom that carries into future games.
- **Weakness:** every reforge trades points later for power now.

### Honeycake Cook

- **Stats this game:** +1 Charm, +1 Luck, -1 Brawn
- **Passive, Forager:** successes at Meadow, Forest, or Creature locations also yield ingredients.
- **Active, Cook (1 stamina):** Hearty Stew (+3 stamina), Courage Pie (+1 die on all rolls next turn), or Sleepy Scones (a rival skips their next action).
- **Signature, The Feast:** all guests restore stamina; you gain 1 VP per player who accepts.
- **Weakness:** your Mishaps eat one ingredient.

## Elemental classes (fully built)

### Rain-Barrel Tidecaller (water)

*"Water always finds a way. Usually into your boots."*

- **Stats this game:** +1 Spark, +1 Charm, -1 Brawn
- **Passive, Flow:** once per turn, roll any option with a different stat than the one listed.
- **Active, Cleanse (1 stamina):** remove a trap, haunt, hex, or curse; if a rival set it, you secretly learn who.
- **Signature, The Great Flood:** every trap washes away, and each rival's least valuable item drifts to you.
- **Weakness:** -1 die at Mountain and Peril locations.

### Moss-Footed Geomancer (earth)

*"I don't move. The world moves around me."*

- **Stats this game:** +2 Brawn (the Charm penalty was removed after Playtest 3)
- **Passive, Rooted:** ignore the first damage each turn; Dig options get +2 dice.
- **Active, Tremor (2 stamina):** rivals at a shared location lose 1 stamina and drop an item; you pick one up.
- **Signature, Mountain Heart:** +1 Brawn and +1 max stamina for the rest of the game.
- **Weakness:** can't push your luck.

### Cinder-Heart Pyromancer (fire)

*"Everything's flammable if you believe in yourself."*

- **Stats this game:** +2 Spark, -1 Luck
- **Passive, Kindling:** each 6 adds a Heat token; at 3 Heat, your next roll gets +3 dice.
- **Active, Burn the Path (2 stamina):** remove a location name from the deck for the whole game.
- **Signature, Phoenix:** the first time you'd drop to 0 health, rise with full health and stamina.
- **Weakness:** your Mishaps burn one of your items.

### Kite-Riding Windcaller (air)

*"Catch me if you can. You can't."*

- **Stats this game:** +1 Wits, +1 Luck, -1 Brawn
- **Passive, Whispers on the Wind:** each round, learn one rival's chosen location before it resolves.
- **Active, Gust (1 stamina):** a rival must take their other teaser card instead.
- **Signature, Cyclone:** collect every chosen explore card, shuffle, and redeal at random.
- **Weakness:** you lose ties in brawls.

### Elemental rivalries

Light counters, not hard rules:

- Water's Cleanse can restore a location Fire burned.
- Earth is Rooted, so it's immune to Air's Gust.
- Air's Whispers can spot a Water player sneaking around.
- Fire's Heat is snuffed out at Swamp locations.

## Concept classes: open questions

These 13 have a name and a hook (see the table) but no full kit yet. Each has one design question to answer before it can be built.

- **Thunder-Oath Knight:** how much bonus per damage taken, and does it reset each turn?
- **Mushroom-Fed Berserker:** does eating loot give dice, stamina, or both, and which mushrooms backfire?
- **Teacup Duelist:** does disarming steal the item or just disable it for a round?
- **Whisper-Debt Assassin:** how are secrets earned, and can they be traded to other players?
- **Mapless Wanderer:** is drawing 3 cards too strong without a matching weakness?
- **Moonlight Wizard:** how often should World cards bring night so the class isn't idle?
- **Ink-Bound Scribe:** does turning Lessons into scrolls slow permanent growth, and is that the trade-off?
- **Starfall Sorcerer:** how far can Spark and Mishap rates rise before the class feels random?
- **Candlewax Warlock:** what is the pact cost: VP, health, or a debt a rival can collect?
- **Rain-Bell Cleric:** what price can a Cleric charge to heal a rival?
- **Root-Sung Druid:** which tag unlocks which form, and how many forms at launch?
- **Off-Key Bard:** which effects do 1s trigger without overlapping the Witch's Mishap Mastery?
- **Clockwork Tinkerer:** how is it different enough from the Blacksmith?
