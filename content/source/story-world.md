# Story: World

The moment each World card arrives, shown big on the screen before play continues. The card's own flavor line and effect appear underneath.

## Weather and Time

### Moonrise
The moon heaves itself over the hills, enormous and close enough to see its craters. Every shadow in Thaloroot grows long, and the thal in the roots starts to glow along with it.

### The Long Night
The sun sets and simply doesn't come back on schedule. Somewhere in the dark, things with teeth stretch and grin, and every lantern in the realm burns a little brighter in defiance.

### The Eclipse
The sun blinks. For one long, strange round, the sky is a black coin rimmed with fire, and every scrap of thal in Thaloroot hums twice as loud.

### Thal Fog
A thick, glittering fog rolls up out of the roots and swallows the paths whole. Adventurers can barely read a signpost, unless they brought a light.

### Gentle Rain
Soft rain falls across the realm, the good kind that smells like green things. The swamps swell and sing; the mountain paths turn to slick, muddy rock.

### Heat Shimmer
The air wobbles like soup. Every step uphill feels like two, armor gets heavy, and somewhere a fire-mage is having the time of their life.

### Thunderstorm
The sky splits with a crack that rattles teeth. Lightning walks across the mountain peaks, and anyone caught in the high places had better be quick or lucky.

### Golden Dawn
The sun comes up gold and gentle, and for one moment everything in Thaloroot looks possible. Aches ease. Breath comes easier. Everyone stands a little straighter.

### Pollen Breeze
A warm wind shakes the meadows, and clouds of glittering pollen drift across the realm. Everybody sneezes. Some of the sneezes sparkle.

### First Frost
Overnight, frost crusts the bogs and silvers the mountain trails. Swamp crossings get easier; the high passes turn treacherous.

### Windy Day
A wild wind tears across Thaloroot, scattering maps, hats, and plans. Every adventurer catches a glimpse of more roads than they meant to.

### Thal Tide
The roots surge. Thal rises like a tide under every field and forest, and every treasure pulled from the ground this round comes up shining brighter.

## Monsters

### The Pickle Wyrm
A smell of vinegar rolls across the realm, followed by a long, green, very hungry shape. The Pickle Wyrm has woken, and it can smell coin from three valleys away.

### The Gloomhound
Two lantern-bright eyes open in the dark between the trees. The Gloomhound pads out of the shadows, sniffing for whoever is carrying the most.

### The Bog Troll King
The swamp heaves, and the Bog Troll King rises dripping from his throne of mud. Every bridge, ford, and lily pad is his now, and he charges a toll.

### The Mimic Chest
Somewhere in Thaloroot, a treasure chest yawns, stretches its little legs, and licks its lid. The next adventurer to find a treasure is going to have company.

### A Swarm of Sprites
A giggling cloud of sprites spills out of a hollow log, each one the size of a thumb and twice as grabby. They're headed for whoever has the most stuff.

### The Hollow Knight
In the ruins, an empty suit of armor creaks to its feet, still loyal to a king long dead. It patrols the old stones, challenging anyone who enters.

### The Thornback Matriarch
The forest goes silent. Then a roar, and the crack of trees snapping. Someone befriended her cub, and the Thornback Matriarch wants to know who.

### The Stormcrow Flock
The sky darkens with crows, a whole flock of them, crackling with static. They circle the mountains and the perilous places, and they know where the storm is going.

### The Hungry Mist
A gray mist creeps out of the deep places, and the birds stop singing. It eats sound first. Then it eats everything else, and it hunts whoever dares go deepest.

## Merchants

### Mrs. Pennywhistle's Rolling Shop
A bell jingles, wheels rattle, and Mrs. Pennywhistle's cart comes trundling over the hill, stacked to the sky with curiosities. "Everything's for sale!" she calls. "Including the cart, for the right price."

### The Mudlark Barge
A flat barge slides out of the reeds, piled with dripping bargains. The mudlarks aboard wave cheerfully. It all smells like the bottom of a pond, because it was.

### The Graftwright Tinker
A Graftwright in goggles sets up a folding workbench at the crossroads, surrounded by humming, half-grafted gadgets. "I can improve that," she says, eyeing your pack. "Probably."

### The Rootkeeper Exchange
A circle of Rootkeepers sits down under an old oak and opens their books. They don't want your coin. They want to see you grow.

### Old Nan's Pie Cart
The smell arrives first: butter, berries, and cinnamon. Old Nan's cart creaks into view. Every pie is delicious. One of them is also very, very sleepy.

### The Shadow Market
Lanterns flicker in an alley that wasn't there yesterday. Hooded sellers murmur prices for secrets, and nobody uses their real name.

### The Traveling Auction
A striped tent goes up in a field overnight, and a fast-talking auctioneer bangs her gavel. One rare treasure, sold to the highest secret bidder. Going once...

## Rumors

### Whispers of Gold
Word travels from tavern to tavern: something glittering has turned up in the old stones. The first adventurer to make it pay in the ruins will find out what.

### The Bog Is Burping
The swamp is burping, loudly, and everyone knows what that means: pearls are coming up with the bubbles.

### Stars on the Mountain
Last night a star fell on the mountain peaks. Shepherds saw it come down glowing. Whoever succeeds up there next might find where it landed.

### A Map Fragment
Half a map turns up in a bottle, pointing somewhere dangerous. The first adventurer bold enough to conquer a three-skull place will find the other half, and the prize.

### The Lost Lamb
A fluffy thal-sheep is loose and very lost. The shepherd is offering a reward to the first adventurer who makes friends with a creature and brings word.

### A Hermit Is Recruiting
A notice is nailed to a tree: a hermit seeks a patient student. The first adventurer to choose a lesson over a reward gets an extra helping of wisdom.

### A Purse for a Brawler
The tavern wants a champion, and it's put up a purse to prove it. The first adventurer to win a Brawl walks away richer.

### The Fairies Are Dancing
The fairies are dancing, and lucky rolls are in the air. The first adventurer to roll a pair of sixes catches a fairy's eye, and a fairy's charm.

### A Ghost Wants Company
Someone in the ruins is lonely. The first adventurer to sit down and rest among the old stones will have a ghostly friend, and a keepsake.

### Taproot Tremors
The ground near the Great Thalowood shivered last night, and the roots are still humming. The next adventurer to conquer the deep forest might find something astonishing in the soil.

## Chaos

### Frog Rain
It's raining frogs. Not metaphorically. They bounce off hats, plop into soup, and some adventurers get a little too close to the transformation.

### Pass It On
A mischievous gust snatches every adventurer's map and shuffles them around. Nobody's going quite where they meant to this round.

### Gravity Hiccup
Everything gets slightly floaty. Rocks drift. Swords wobble. Muscles are useless; magic soars.

### The Giggles
A giggling fit sweeps across Thaloroot and won't stop. Nobody can keep a straight face long enough to pick a fight.

### Squirrel Heist
A blur of tails tears through every camp in Thaloroot at once. When the dust settles, everyone's pack is lighter, and a stash of stolen goods waits somewhere in the forest.

### Shoe Swap
Everyone wakes up wearing someone else's shoes, and carrying something that isn't theirs. Nobody knows how it happened. Nobody ever does.

### Bloom Burst
The roots sigh, and the whole realm sparkles. Thal drifts up from the ground like dandelion fluff, and coins turn up in every pocket.

### Talking Animals
Every animal in Thaloroot can suddenly talk, and they have opinions. Companions lean in close to share what they've overheard about everyone else's quests.

### The Wandering Door
A door appears in the middle of a field, standing all by itself. It's slightly ajar. Light glows through the crack. Anyone could open it.

### Backwards Day
Everyone wakes up on the wrong side of the Bloom. The sun rises in the west, the rivers run uphill, and everyone takes their turn in the opposite order.

### Thal Hiccup
The magic skips a beat. Every enchanted tool, every grafted gadget, every lucky charm sputters and goes quiet for a round.

### Festival of Lanterns
Lanterns float up from every village in Thaloroot, filling the sky with warm light. Anyone carrying a light of their own is welcomed like a hero.

## Escalation

### The Sky Cracks
A crack splits the sky from horizon to horizon, and through it pours light the color of thal. Everything is worth more now. Everything costs more, too.

### The Dragon Stirs
Deep under the mountain, the Dragon rolls over in its sleep, and every monster in Thaloroot gets bolder. Something very large is waking up.

### Masks Appear
Masks appear on every doorstep overnight: feathered, gilded, smiling. Nobody knows who left them. Nobody can tell who's wearing them.

### The Roots Tremble
The ground shudders, and the deepest roots of the realm begin to stir. Treasures pulled from the most dangerous places come up heavy with thal.

### The Gardener-Kings Stir
In the ruins, the statues turn their heads. The ghosts of the Gardener-Kings are waking, and they guard their old treasures jealously, but generously reward the brave.

### The Bloom Peaks
The Bloom reaches its height. Every flower is open, every root is full, and every success in Thaloroot comes with a little extra gold.

## Finales

### The Sky Falls
The crack in the sky splits wide, and stars begin to fall across Thaloroot like burning seeds. One last chance to catch one before the Bloom ends.

### The Dragon Wakes
The mountain splits open, and the Dragon rises into the sky, roaring fire. Every adventurer in the realm turns to face it. This is the last fight of the Bloom.

### The Midnight Masquerade
At midnight, the masks come off. Every adventurer gathers in the ruins for a grand masquerade, and every secret in the realm is about to be revealed.

### The Taproot Wakes
The ground splits, and the Taproot itself rises from the deep, glowing and enormous. Anyone brave enough might reach out and touch the heart of all thal.

### The Last Court
The ghosts of the Gardener-Kings take their thrones one last time and hold court over the realm. They judge every adventurer's haul, and they love a treasure.

### The Great Harvest
The Bloom bursts into a final, glorious harvest. For one last round, every reward in Thaloroot comes twice.

### The Bloom Fades
The thal sinks back into the roots, slow and gentle, like a long exhale. Every adventurer feels a last wave of strength for the final turn.

### Last Light
The sun sets on the Bloom, and darkness falls over Thaloroot. Only the lights adventurers carry still shine, and they shine like treasures.

### The Grand Bazaar
Every merchant in Thaloroot arrives at once for one final, glorious market. The rarest treasures of the Bloom are for sale, one last time.
