# World Cards

World cards run the world and set the game's length. At setup, the group chooses how long to play, which sets the deck size. Each game deals that deck from a larger pool, and when it runs out, everyone takes one final turn.

## Building the deck

1. Choose a game length: Quick, Standard, or Long.
2. For each category, draw that length's number of cards at random from the category's pool.
3. Shuffle them together. Escalation cards go only into the second half of the deck.
4. Place the Finale card at the bottom.
5. At the end of each round, draw one World card. A rotating World Keeper reads it aloud; in the digital version it plays on the TV.

| Category | Pool | Quick (15 cards, about 60 min) | Standard (20 cards, about 90 min) | Long (25 cards, about 2 hrs) | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weather and Time | 12 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Shifts which classes shine |
| Monsters | 9 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Shared threats, often aimed at the leader |
| Merchants | 7 | 2 | 3 | 3 | Gives hoarded coin a purpose |
| Rumors | 10 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Creates races and reveals intentions |
| Chaos | 12 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Pure whimsy and story moments |
| Escalation | 6 | 2 | 2 | 3 | Raises the stakes and foreshadows the finale |
| Finale | 9 | 1 | 1 | 1 | The last card; sets up the final turn |

Each 5 cards adds roughly 30 minutes. Counts per category stay fixed for a given length; only the specific cards change. Play times are estimates to confirm in playtesting.

## Ending the game

- The Finale card is always the last World card drawn. Its effect shapes the final turn, then every player takes one last turn and the game is scored.
- When the first player completes their mission, the Finale moves up so only 3 World cards remain before it in a Quick game, 5 in Standard, and 7 in Long. Cards it skips are discarded unseen. If fewer cards than that remain, it stays where it is.
- Everyone can see how many cards remain, so the countdown is always public.

## Choosing the finale

The finale pays off what the game set up. If any Escalation card was drawn, the finale matches the most recent one. If none was drawn, one of the three general finales is chosen at random.

| Escalation (mid-game, rest of game) | Matching finale |
| --- | --- |
| **The Sky Cracks:** all rewards are worth +1 VP, but every option costs +1 stamina | **The Sky Falls:** each player picks one last location; any success also earns a Fallen Star (10 / 5) |
| **The Dragon Stirs:** Monster cards need 1 more success to defeat | **The Dragon Wakes:** a final boss anyone can attack with Brawn or Spark; each success earns 1 VP, and the killing blow earns 5 more |
| **Masks Appear:** players at a Meeting don't learn who they're meeting until it resolves | **The Midnight Masquerade:** all mission guesses are worth double |
| **The Roots Tremble:** treasures found at three-skull locations are worth +1 VP | **The Taproot Wakes:** on the final turn, any player may reach for the Taproot instead of exploring (Spark, 4 successes, all stamina); success earns 8 VP |
| **The Gardener-Kings Stir:** Ruins options need 1 more success, but Ruins treasures are worth +2 VP | **The Last Court:** the ghostly Gardener-Kings judge the realm; the player holding the most valuable single treasure gains 6 VP, and everyone else holding a treasure gains 2 |
| **The Bloom Peaks:** every success also grants 1 coin | **The Great Harvest:** on the final turn, every success gives double rewards |

**General finales**

| Finale | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **The Bloom Fades** | Every player restores all stamina before the final turn |
| **Last Light** | At scoring, each light source held is worth +2 VP |
| **The Grand Bazaar** | Before the final turn, the market opens to everyone with a merchant stocking 2 rare items |

## Card anatomy

Every World card has a category, a duration, one line of flavor, and an effect. Durations are Instant, This round, Until cleared, or Rest of game.

## The card pool

65 cards: 56 regular cards plus 9 finales. The 6 Escalation cards and all 9 finales are listed under Choosing the finale above. Cards marked Night count as night for class abilities such as the Moonlight Wizard and Lantern-Hour Thief.

### Monsters (9)

A monster hunts one player each round. At the start of that player's next turn, before exploring, they must choose one of the monster's options. Unless a card says otherwise, a monster leaves after 3 rounds if no one defeats it.

| Monster | Hunts | Options | Reward for defeating it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Pickle Wyrm** *(It smells coin. It always smells coin.)* | The player with the most coin | Fight (Brawn, 3 successes), Bribe (pay 3 coin), or Flee (lose 2 stamina) | Wyrm Scale (6 / 3) |
| **The Gloomhound** *(Its eyes glow like lanterns. It wants yours.)* | The player holding the most items | Fight (Brawn, 2 successes), Tame (Charm, 2 successes; befriends it as a companion), or Flee (lose a random item) | Moonfang (6 / 3), or the Gloomhound itself if tamed |
| **The Bog Troll King** *(He owns the bog. Ask him.)* | Every player who picks a Swamp location | Fight (Brawn, 3 successes), Pay the Toll (2 coin), or Answer His Riddle (Wits, 2 successes) | Troll King's Club (gear: +1 die on Brawn rolls) |
| **The Mimic Chest** *(That treasure has teeth.)* | The next player who finds a treasure | Fight (Brawn, 2 successes) or lose that treasure | 8 coin |
| **A Swarm of Sprites** *(Tiny, giggling, and very grabby.)* | The player holding the most items; leaves after 2 rounds | Swat (Luck, 2 successes), pay 1 Spark token, or lose a random item. Anyone may scatter them as their explore (Spark, 2 successes) | 2 Spark tokens |
| **The Hollow Knight** *(Empty armor, still loyal to a dead king.)* | Every player who picks a Ruins location | Duel (Brawn, 3 successes) or Find Its Weak Point (Wits, 2 successes); losing costs 2 health | Hollow Helm (rare gear: +1 max health) |
| **The Thornback Matriarch** *(Someone befriended her cub.)* | The player with the most companions | Fight (Brawn, 3 successes), Calm (Charm, 3 successes), or Flee (one companion flees) | Matriarch's Claw (8 / 4) |
| **The Stormcrow Flock** *(They know where the storm is going.)* | Every player at a Mountain or Peril location; leaves after 2 rounds | Lose 1 stamina, or Read Their Flight (Wits, 2 successes) to see the next World card instead. Anyone may drive them off (Spark, 2 successes) | Stormcrow Feather (6 / 3; one use: see the next World card) |
| **The Hungry Mist** *(It eats sound first. Then everything else.)* | The player who picked the highest-skull location this round | Fight (Spark, 3 successes) or Flee (lose 2 health) | Mist Pearl (8 / 4) |

## Open questions

- Do the estimated play times hold up at 2, 4, and 6 players?
- Monsters that hunt the richest player act as catch-up. Playtest whether that feels fair or punishing.
- Should light sources get a small bonus on Night cards (Moonrise, the Long Night, the Eclipse)?
