Missions

A pool of 51 draft missions, 17 at each difficulty. Each player is dealt 3 and secretly keeps 1.

How missions are built

  • Three milestones, claimed secretly in any order, each worth partial credit.

  • A finale, available once all three milestones are claimed.

  • A flourish, an optional bonus worth 2 VP.

  • Milestones use tags, not specific cards, so random draws can't strand a player.

DifficultyMilestonesFinaleTotal with flourish
One skull3 + 3 + 4921
Two skulls3 + 4 + 41225
Three skulls4 + 5 + 51632

These values were raised for Playtest 3 and rebalanced for Playtest 4 (see the Playtest Log) so that missions, not kept treasure, are the biggest source of VP.

Finale grace: once all three milestones are claimed, your finale's location requirement drops by one skull. A three-skull finale can be done at a two-skull or harder location, and a two-skull finale at any location with the right tag.

The path becomes clear: once all three milestones are claimed, draw 3 explore teasers each turn instead of 2 until you finish the finale.

One-skull missions

Moth Queen's Lullaby (Meadow, Creature)

The Moth Queen can't sleep, and when she can't sleep, nobody in the Meadow can.

  • Succeed at a Meadow location (3 VP).

  • Choose a rest or no-roll option twice (3 VP).

  • Befriend a creature (4 VP).

  • Finale: while holding a companion, succeed on a Charm option at a Meadow location (9 VP).

  • Flourish: never choose Brawl at a Meeting (2 VP).

The Rootkeeper's Errand (Forest, Ruins)

The Rootkeepers need something returned to the old stones, and they don't trust Graftwrights to do it.

  • Succeed at a Forest location (3 VP).

  • Choose Ally at a Meeting (3 VP).

  • Find a treasure at a Ruins location (4 VP).

  • Finale: while holding a treasure found at Ruins, succeed at a two-skull Forest location (9 VP).

  • Flourish: lend Assist dice to a rival at least once (2 VP).

Blue Ribbon at the Bloom Fair (Meadow, Forest)

The judges only care about one thing: who brought the most marvelous haul.

  • Find a treasure at a Meadow location (3 VP).

  • Find a treasure at a Forest location (3 VP).

  • Buy an item from a Merchant (4 VP).

  • Finale: start your turn holding treasures with a total coin value of 12 or more (9 VP).

  • Flourish: end the game holding 4 or more treasures (2 VP).

The Frog Prince's Pond (Swamp, Creature)

A frog in the bog claims he's royalty. The only way to know for sure is to become one.

  • Succeed at a Swamp location (3 VP).

  • Be turned into a frog by any means (3 VP).

  • Befriend a creature at a Swamp location (4 VP).

  • Finale: succeed on a Charm option at a two-skull Swamp location (9 VP).

  • Flourish: be a frog when the game ends (2 VP).

Mushroom Census (Forest, Swamp)

  • Succeed at a Swamp location (3 VP).

  • Finale: succeed on a Wits option at a two-skull Forest or Swamp location (9 VP).

  • Flourish: claim every milestone without pushing your luck (2 VP).

  • Flourish: no companion flees all game (2 VP).

  • Flourish: end the game holding 15 or more coin (2 VP).
